                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \section*{\centering \color{headerblue} COURSE CONTENT}

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \subsection*{UNIT I: Introduction and Archimedes Principle}
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \noindent Introduction: Basic Concepts and Definitions, Distinction between a fluid and a solid; Density, Specific weight, Specific gravity, Kinematic and dynamic viscosity; variation of viscosity with temperature, Newton’s law of viscosity; Classification of fluids. 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \noindent Fluid Statics: Fluid Pressure: Pressure at a point, Pascal’s law, Piezometer, U-Tube Manometer, Differential Manometer.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \subsection*{UNIT II: Fluid Kinematics}
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \noindent Classification of flows, stream line, streak line, path line, continuity equation.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \subsection*{UNIT III: Fluid Dynamics}
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \noindent Surface and body forces - Euler’s and Bernoulli’s equations for flow along a stream line, momentum equation and its applications.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \subsection*{UNIT IV: Boundary Layer Concepts}
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \noindent Definition, thicknesses, characteristics along thin plate, laminar and turbulent boundary layers.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \subsection*{UNIT V: Flow Through Pipes}
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            \noindent Reynolds experiment, Darcy Weisbach equation, Minor losses in pipes, pipes in series and pipes in parallel, total energy line-hydraulic gradient line.
